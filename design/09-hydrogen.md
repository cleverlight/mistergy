## Topics to Cover

- **Sulfur–Iodine (S–I) thermochemical cycle:**
  - Three reactions, minimum process temperature ~850 °C
  - Thermal efficiency (~40–50% HHV projected)
  - Corrosive acid handling (H₂SO₄, HI) and material requirements
  - Heat coupling to reactor via IHX
- **High-Temperature Electrolysis (HTE / SOEC):**
  - Solid oxide electrolyzer cells
  - Operates at 700–900 °C; lower temperature requirement than S–I
  - Combined heat + electricity input; efficiency ~45–55% HHV
  - Technology readiness level comparison with S–I
- Hydrogen output rate (kg/day) as a function of thermal power
- Safety: hydrogen inventory management, leak detection
- Integration with nuclear heat delivery temperature
