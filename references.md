## Key Design Studies

- **GT-MHR** (General Atomics, ~2004): 600 MW(th) prismatic annular core, direct helium Brayton cycle. LaBar, M.P. et al., "The Gas Turbine-Modular Helium Reactor," GA-A24235, General Atomics. Primary reference for annular prismatic geometry and direct Brayton integration.
- **PBMR** (South Africa, 2006–2010): 400 MW(th) pebble-bed direct Brayton; extensive turbomachine and precooler design work.
- **NGNP** (US DOE, 2004–2011): National project to advance VHTR to commercial demonstration. Idaho National Laboratory. Final report: *NGNP Project 2011 Status and Path Forward*, INL/EXT-11-23907.
- **Xe-100** (X-energy): 200 MW(th) pebble-bed SMR pursuing NRC licensing under 10 CFR Part 50. Modern reference for TRISO supply chain and regulatory approach.

## TRISO Fuel Performance

- Petti, D.A. et al. (2004). *TRISO-Coated Particle Fuel Phenomenon Identification and Ranking Tables (PIRTs) for Fission Product Transport Due to Manufacturing, Operations, and Accidents*. NUREG/CR-6844, ORNL/TM-2003/223. Idaho National Laboratory / Oak Ridge National Laboratory. — Comprehensive failure mechanism analysis underlying the 1600 °C design limit.
- Demkowicz, P.A. et al. (2015). "Irradiation performance of AGR-1 TRISO fuel." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 306, 1–12. — US AGR-1 irradiation test results; TRISO performance data at high burnup under US qualification programme.
- Petti, D.A. et al. (2003). "Key differences in the fabrication, irradiation and safety testing of US and German TRISO-coated particle fuel, and their implications on fuel performance." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 222, 281–297. — Comparative analysis of US and German TRISO fabrication standards.
- Kim, Y.W. et al. (2009). "Performance of TRISO-coated fuel particles with UO₂ kernels." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 239, 814–822. — Korean irradiation performance data; confirms coating integrity to high burnup.

## Graphite Moderator and Reflector

- Burchell, T.D. (1996). "Radiation effects in graphite." In *Carbon Materials for Advanced Technologies*, Elsevier. — Foundational reference for fast-neutron-induced dimensional change and property evolution in nuclear graphite.
- Nightingale, R.E. (1962). *Nuclear Graphite*. Academic Press, New York. — Classic reference; irradiation effects, Wigner energy release.
- Marsden, B.J. et al. (2016). "Nuclear graphite for high temperature reactors." In *Handbook of Generation IV Nuclear Reactors*, Woodhead Publishing. — Modern summary of graphite specification, irradiation behaviour, lifetime management.
- Shibata, T. et al. (2008). "Mechanical property changes of nuclear graphite IG-110 and IG-430 under neutron irradiation." *Journal of Nuclear Science and Technology*, 45(5), 509–514. — IG-110 dimensional change data directly applicable to this design.

## Thermal-Hydraulics: TPMS Geometry

- Al-Ketan, O. and Abu Al-Rub, R.K. (2019). "Multifunctional mechanical metamaterials based on triply periodic minimal surface lattices." *Advanced Engineering Materials*, 21(10), 1900524. — Physical and mechanical properties of TPMS architectures including gyroid.

- Kaur, I. and Singh, P. (2021). "State-of-the-art in heat exchanger additive manufacturing." *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 178, 121600. — Review of additive-manufacture-native heat transfer geometries.
- Peng, H. et al. (2022). "Gyroid sheet TPMS heat exchanger performance characterization and comparison." *Applied Thermal Engineering*, 218, 119296. — Direct gyroid performance data in turbulent flow; reports Nu enhancement consistent with §05 estimates.

## SiC/SiC Ceramic Matrix Composites and Ceramic Turbomachinery

- Naslain, R. (2004). "Design, preparation and properties of non-oxide CMCs for application in engines and nuclear reactors: an overview." *Composites Science and Technology*, 64, 155–170. — Foundational CMC design reference; SiC/SiC fabrication routes and properties.
- Zok, F.W. (2016). "Ceramic-matrix composites enable revolutionary gains in turbine engine efficiency." *American Ceramic Society Bulletin*, 95(5), 22–28. — Engineering rationale for SiC/SiC in turbine rotors; property comparison with Ni superalloys.
- Grujicic, M. et al. (2006). "Finite element analysis of the effect of coating deposited via CVD on cracking behaviour of SiC/SiC composites." *Journal of Materials Science*, 41, 1581–1590. — CVD SiC coating process relevant to TRISO and CMC fabrication.
- Continuous-fibre SiC/SiC turbine components: GE Aviation and CFM LEAP engine programmes (2015 onwards) demonstrated SiC/SiC CMC turbine shrouds and combustor liners; current development work on rotating blades.

## Passive Decay Heat Removal / RCCS

- Takeda, T. et al. (2004). "Safety demonstration tests using high-temperature engineering test reactor." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 233, 301–308. — HTTR DLOFC test results; passive RCCS performance at reactor scale.
- Sato, H. et al. (2010). "Safety design and evaluation of inherent safety reactor characteristics of the HTTR." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 240, 2492–2503. — Quantified passive decay heat removal capacity of air-cooled RCCS for a 30 MW(th) prismatic HTGR.
- Morita, K. and Fukuda, K. (1995). "Passive decay heat removal from the HTTR core." *Nuclear Engineering and Design*, 157, 347–358. — Air-side natural convection analysis applicable to RCCS sizing methodology.

## Helium Brayton Cycle and Turbomachinery

- Wright, S.A. et al. (2010). *Overview and Results of the Supercritical CO₂ Bypass Flow Test Loop*. Sandia National Laboratories, SAND2010-9901C. — Closed Brayton cycle component performance reference (CO₂ rather than He, but turbomachine scaling methodology identical).
- McDonald, C.F. (1995). "Gas turbine recuperator technology advancements." *ASME International Gas Turbine and Aeroengine Congress*, Paper 95-GT-71. — Compact recuperator design for helium service; PCHE performance data.
- Van Ravenswaay, J.P. et al. (2008). *PBMR Closed Cycle Gas Turbine Power Plant Simulation*. PBMR Ltd. — Direct helium Brayton cycle mass balance and efficiency analysis at comparable scale.

## Neutronics: LEU HTGR
