## Key Design Studies

- **GT-MHR**: General Atomics gas turbine modular helium reactor, 600 MW(th) prismatic, direct Brayton cycle — primary reference for large prismatic VHTR
- **PBMR** (South Africa): 400 MW(th) pebble-bed modular reactor — reference for pebble-bed power conversion
- **NGNP** (US DOE): National project 2004–2011, extensive IHX and hydrogen coupling studies; final report 2011
- **Xe-100** (X-energy): Modern 200 MW(th) pebble-bed SMR pursuing NRC licensing
